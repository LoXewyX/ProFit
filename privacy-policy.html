ProFit Privacy Policy

ProFit is an Android fitness app and a web app. It is designed to let you manage your routines and progress without creating an account or sending your workout data to the developer’s servers.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In summary: ProFit does not collect or share with the developer the data you enter into the app. Routines, sessions, measurements and preferences are stored locally on your device. The website is static and does not use its own analytics or tracking tools; however, the hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes.

1. Controller and contact

App: ProFit.

Developer: Luis Ruiz Caracuel (LoXewyX).

For questions about this policy or privacy, you can open an issue in the official GitHub repository. Please do not include personal data in public issues.

Privacy contact via GitHub Issues

2. Data handled by the app

ProFit does not require registration or a user account. We do not operate our own server or API to receive your personal or workout data.

The app may save information that you enter or edit in your device’s local storage, such as:

  • Routines and selected exercises.
  • Workout sessions and activity history.
  • Measurements and tracking data you choose to enter.
  • App preferences.

This data stays on the device where you use ProFit and is not transmitted to the developer. The app does not use it to create advertising profiles or track you across apps or websites.

3. Data collection, use and transmission

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share workout data stored in ProFit. The app does not deliberately include its own advertising, analytics or user-tracking tools.

The ProFit website at loxewyx.github.io/ProFit/ has no forms for collecting personal data and does not use its own analytics or advertising tools. If you follow external links, such as to GitHub or Google Play, the relevant services’ privacy policies apply.

4. Website hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a GitHub service. Although the developer does not receive personal data through the website, GitHub may process technical visit information, including IP addresses, to maintain the security and integrity of its service. This processing is controlled by GitHub and subject to its own policies.

The app is also distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may process data related to your account, device, installation, security or download under its own terms and policies. This processing is handled by Google, not by ProFit’s local storage.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

ProFit does not deliberately set its own tracking cookies or use advertising pixels or its own analytics systems. The hosting provider and linked external services may use their own technologies or technical logs, subject to their respective policies.

6. Retention and deletion of local data

Workout data and preferences remain in local storage until you delete them or clear the app’s data. The developer does not keep a copy on its servers.

  • Android: you can clear the data in the system settings under Apps → ProFit → Storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names may vary by device.
  • Web: if the browser saves local data while you use the web version, you can remove it in the browser settings by clearing the data for the relevant site.

Once local data is deleted, the developer has no copy from which to restore it.

7. Security

Because workout data remains on your device, its protection also depends on your device’s security, screen lock and any backup settings you have enabled. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date and protecting access to your device.

8. Children’s privacy

ProFit does not deliberately request children’s personal data and does not require an account. Users, or their legal guardians where appropriate, should avoid entering unnecessary personal information.

9. Open-source code

ProFit’s source code is public and can be viewed or downloaded from the GitHub repository. You can review how the app works and what data it stores locally.

View and download ProFit source code on GitHub

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if ProFit’s features, dependencies or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available on this page and will state its latest update date.

This policy applies to the ProFit Android app and web version, except for external services governed by their own terms.
